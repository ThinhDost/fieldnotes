What byte entropy can tell you

Notes on interpreting byte entropy and the limits of using it to describe data.

entropypythondata-analysis

On this page

  • Research question
  • Method
  • Limitations

Research question

What does the entropy of a byte sequence reveal about its distribution? This example uses synthetic data for illustration.

Method

The sample contains two values that occur equally often, so the theoretical entropy is one bit per byte.

Limitations

This measurement uses frequencies. Two samples with the same byte distribution and different ordering can have identical entropy. A conclusion about format or provenance needs additional evidence.
